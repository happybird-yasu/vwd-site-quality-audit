* Net migration = people who moved in − people who moved out. Positive = net inflow of people; negative = net outflow (unit: persons)
Source: Statistics Bureau of Japan, “Report on Internal Migration in Japan” (e-Stat)
Calculation method:
• Sum of the net migration figures of the prefectures included in Chugoku
• Three years compared: 2019, 2021 and 2025
• The national total is theoretically zero (domestic moves cancel out across the country)
